# Code of Conduct

Slice Media is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

Be considerate, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, respect different levels
of experience, and keep discussion focused on improving the project. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sustained disruption, publication of another person's private information, and other
unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community. Report conduct concerns privately to `hallo@slicemedia.de`. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the person reporting.
